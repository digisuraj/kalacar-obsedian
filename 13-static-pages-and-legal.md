# 13 — Store Information and Policies

← [[10-razorpay-payments]] · next → [[14-security-seo-performance]]

Keep these pages short and factual. Use owner-provided business details and policies; do not invent legal terms, delivery promises, contact information, spiritual claims, or customer guarantees.

## Essential pages

- **About:** a brief owner-approved description of the small idol shop and 3D-printing/finishing process, using real images if available.
- **Contact:** owner-provided support email and any other contact channel the owner chooses. A simple form is optional.
- **Shipping:** explain owner-managed packing/dispatch and the confirmed delivery estimate/charges. No courier API is needed.
- **Returns/refunds:** state the owner-confirmed cancellation, damage, return, and refund process.
- **Privacy:** explain only the customer, address, order, sign-in, and payment-reference data actually collected, and how customers can contact the owner.
- **Terms:** concise purchase terms based on owner-provided business and legal details.

Link the relevant policy pages from checkout and the footer. Leave launch-blocking details as a checklist item; do not publish placeholders as if they were real policy.

## Not part of v1

No page builder, complex CMS, grievance workflow, marketing capture, social embeds, or map embed. A plain map link can be added if the owner provides one and wants it.
