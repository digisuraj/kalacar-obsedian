# Kalacar UI Direction

## Brand and products

Design a small, owner-run shop for 3D-printed Hindu deity idols and devotional decor: Ganpati/Ganesha, Shankar, Krishna, and related figures. This is not an apparel shop, general marketplace, or large retail platform.

Use a calm, respectful, warm visual style. Let the product photography lead: clean neutral or warm backgrounds, clear close-ups of print/finish, and a scale reference where useful. Avoid caricature, disrespectful poses, loud sale graphics, fake scarcity, or generic fashion-store imagery. Do not invent religious claims or product materials; use the owner's verified product details.

## Storefront: keep it focused

- Header with Kalacar name, Shop, cart, and account access.
- Home page: one strong hero image/message, a small set of product/collection links, a few featured idols, and a compact footer.
- Shop page: simple responsive product grid with photo, name, price, and stock/sold-out state.
- Product page: gallery, price, dimensions, material, finish, care, stock, delivery pincode check, Add to Cart, and clear shipping/return links.
- Cart and checkout: readable order summary, address/pincode form, payment, and clear success/error states.
- Account: email/password and Google sign-in, order history, and saved address only if needed.
- Mobile-first, accessible controls, readable type, and fast-loading images.

Skip sliders unless the owner has multiple prepared hero assets and wants them. No trust badge wall, countdown timers, fake visitor counts, forced popups, social feed, or endless promotional sections.

## Product content

Show factual information that helps someone choose an idol: height/width, material, finish, included items, care, stock, and real product photos. Clearly distinguish the pictured item from any size or color variation. Show discounts only when the owner has set a genuine MRP and offer.

## Owner dashboard

Keep `/admin` visually separate from the public shop. Use a plain, compact order list and order detail with payment and manual fulfilment status. Make the main action obvious: see a new paid order, prepare it, then mark it dispatched and optionally add a tracking note. Avoid charts and settings the owner does not need.

## Content tone

Use concise, warm, respectful language. Avoid claims such as “blessed,” “energized,” or guaranteed spiritual outcomes unless explicitly approved and substantiated by the owner. Do not add Instagram handles/reels, Google Business Profile, or unrelated marketing content without a request.
